About

Family-run for four generations since 1932, Kamogawa-kan offers tatami rooms overlooking the Kamo River in central Kyoto. Guests enjoy private onsen baths, seasonal kaiseki dinners, and traditional hospitality, a short walk from Gion and Pontocho Alley.
